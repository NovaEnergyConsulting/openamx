# Commercial Licence Overview

This document provides a summary of the commercial licensing option available for this project.

The definitive commercial licence is a separately executed written agreement between Nova Energy Consulting Pty Ltd and the licensee.

## Purpose

This project is made available under an open-source AGPLv3 licence and an alternative commercial licence.

The commercial licence allows organisations to use, modify, distribute and integrate the software without being subject to the source-code disclosure obligations of the AGPLv3.

## Typical Commercial Rights

Depending on the agreement, a commercial licence may permit:

- Internal organisational use
- Proprietary modifications
- Closed-source derivative works
- Commercial distribution
- Integration into proprietary products
- Redistribution to customers
- SaaS and hosted service offerings
- Additional support arrangements

## Commercial Licence Benefits

Commercial licensees may additionally receive:

- Commercial support
- Priority issue resolution
- Maintenance releases
- Extended documentation
- Training services
- Consulting services
- Custom development services

## Restrictions

Commercial licences do not transfer ownership of the software.

Unless explicitly agreed in writing, the licence does not:

- Transfer intellectual property ownership
- Permit sublicensing
- Allow trademark usage
- Grant patent ownership rights

## Warranty and Liability

Any warranties, service levels, support commitments and liability limitations are governed solely by the executed commercial agreement.
