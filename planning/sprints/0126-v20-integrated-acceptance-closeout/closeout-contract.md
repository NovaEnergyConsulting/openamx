## Required Status Model

Record four separate concepts for each requirement:

1. **Raw status:** `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED/UNAVAILABLE`, or `NOT RUN`.
2. **Builder interpretation:** covered behavior, evidence strength and risk.
3. **Residual:** exact row/condition, owner, downstream impact and artifact.
4. **Product Owner disposition:** separately accepted/closed, accepted with
   residuals, or blocked/open.

An accepted sprint with residuals never turns an underlying raw FAIL,
BLOCKED/UNAVAILABLE or NOT RUN into PASS.
