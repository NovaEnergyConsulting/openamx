Battery-resistor DC circuit

This schematic shows a 9 V source and a 15 ohm resistor. The indicated
conventional current is constant at 9 / 15 = 0.6 A. The red
arrow moves clockwise along the upper wire to indicate direction; its motion
does not represent a changing current magnitude.

let supplyVoltage: Number = 9
let resistance: Number = 15
let current: Number = supplyVoltage / resistance
fn indicatorX(t: Number): Number = 58 + 25 * t
